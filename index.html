Methodology & Open Data

Data, Active Built-Up Area & Corrected Street Lengths

  1. Active Built-Up Urban Area (built_area_km2): Instead of relying on abstract municipal borders (which often include vast farmland or desert), each city's urban roads are projected onto an equal-area 250m × 250m grid. Only cells with intensive urban street activity (≥ 2 distinct named streets or ≥ 300m of roads) are counted as built-up area (clamped to municipal area muni_area_km2).
  2. Dual-Carriageway Length Correction: Restricted to true urban street classes (primary, secondary, tertiary, residential, unclassified, living_street, pedestrian — excluding service, footway, cycleway, track, motorway, trunk). One-way segments on divided arterial roads (primary/secondary/tertiary) are weighted 0.5× so dual-carriageway boulevards are not double-counted.
  3. Standardized inclusion: localities with population ≥ 10,000 and ≥ 20 normalized streets, with config-driven multilingual affix stripping (including Serbian/Macedonian Cyrillic↔Latin unification), intra-city deduplication, and automatic removal of nested/duplicate overlapping sub-district polygons (≥ 88% exact street containment within a parent city).

Rarity Formulas & Scaling Laws

  • Sqrt (Default): 1 / √n (0–100% city mean). Unique street = 100%, 4 cities = 50%, 100 cities = 10%.
  • Normalized IDF: 1 - log(n) / log(C), adjusting for the number of cities C in each country.
  • Log: 1 / log₂(1 + n) — gentler decay.
  • Length-Weighted Rarity & Boulevard Gap: Weights each street by its dual-carriageway-corrected length. Positive gap (count − length > 0) means longer avenues are more generic; negative gap means longer roads are more unique.
  • Power-Law Scaling: Log-log OLS fits on active built-up area: streets ~ built_area^α, population ~ built_area^γ, and streets ~ population^β.
