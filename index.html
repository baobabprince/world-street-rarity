Methodology & Open Data

Data & Standardized City Scope

  1. City boundaries, populations, and named road segments are queried directly from Overture Maps (OpenStreetMap + open divisions) via DuckDB.
  2. To ensure apples-to-apples comparisons across countries with very different municipal structures (e.g., 35,000 tiny communes in France vs. ~1,200 in Italy), each country includes municipalities/localities with population ≥ 10,000 and ≥ 20 normalized streets.
  3. Language-specific prefixes (Via, Rue de la, Calle de, ul., רחוב), suffixes, and German fused compounds (Goethestraße → goethe) are stripped; historical dates (Via 20 Settembre, 14 Juillet) are preserved; purely numbered streets are tracked separately (numbered_pct); intra-city duplicates are counted once per city.

Rarity Formulas (Default: Square Root)

  • Sqrt (Default): 1 / √n (city mean scaled 0–100%). A street unique to one city scores 100%; appearing in 4 cities scores 50%; in 100 cities scores 10%.
  • Normalized IDF: 1 - log(n) / log(C), where C is the number of cities in that country — adjusts for country size so a street in 10% of cities scores comparably across small and large countries.
  • Log: 1 / log₂(1 + n) — softer decay for widespread names.
  • Pure Exclusive %: Share of a city's streets appearing in only that single city nationwide (n = 1).
